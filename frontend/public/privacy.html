Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 September 2026

HalkaBite uses the information you provide to create and secure your account, process food orders, provide delivery and customer support, and improve the service.

Google Sign-In

If you choose Google Sign-In, we receive your verified email address, name, profile image and Google account identifier. We use this information only to authenticate you and create or link your HalkaBite account. We do not receive your Google password or access your Google Drive, contacts, email, or other Google services.

Information we collect

We may store account and contact details, order and payment status, delivery address, support messages, active login-session device information, IP address, and security audit events.

Sharing and retention

Information is shared only with restaurants, delivery personnel and service providers as needed to provide the service or meet legal and security obligations. We do not sell personal information. Data is retained only as long as necessary for service, security and legal purposes.

Your choices

You can update your profile, sign out active devices, or request account and data deletion by contacting the support email shown in the application.

Security and changes

We use access controls, secure tokens and encrypted connections. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Material changes to this policy will be reflected on this page.
